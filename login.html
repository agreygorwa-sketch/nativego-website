Log in

Welcome back. Sign in to see your requests, chat with your guide, and leave reviews after your trips.

This form runs on the live system. Nothing you type here is saved — it shows what logging in asks for. After checking your answers, it takes you to the live log-in page, where your password is checked against the stored account.

New to NativeGo? Create an account — it takes about a minute, and you can sign up as a tourist or as a local provider.

Cannot log in?

Check that you are using the email address you signed up with. Passwords are case sensitive.

If you still cannot get in, call the helpline on +254 712 345 678 (Monday to Saturday).
