Request a guide

A request is how a tourist books a guide. You pick a guide, a date, and how many people are coming. The guide accepts or declines. That is the whole booking flow.

How booking works — 4 steps

  1. Log in. You need an account to send a request, so the guide knows who you are. Log in or create an account first.
  2. Pick a guide. Choose one of the verified guides below. Open their profile to check their services, price, and reviews.
  3. Send the request. On the guide's page, enter the date, number of travellers, meeting point, and a short note. The request is saved as pending and the guide is told.
  4. Wait for the answer. The guide accepts or declines. If they accept, you message each other here to agree the details. You can see all your requests under My requests on the live system.

The request moves through states: pending → accepted or declined → completed → reviewed. Every step is checked on the live system, so a request cannot skip from pending straight to reviewed.

Why the request is sent on the live system: sending a request changes the database (it creates a row in the requests table), and only a logged-in tourist may do that. This page is the front of the shop; the live system is the till.

What a request contains

When you send a request, the system stores: the service type, a description, the meeting point, the time, the service date, and the number of guests — and the status (pending at first). The guide sees it on their dashboard and accepts or declines. Your request history is kept, so you can look back at past trips.

No payment is taken when you send a request. Payment happens later, through M-Pesa, once you agree with your guide.
