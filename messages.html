Messages

Once you have a request, you and the guide message each other on that request. You agree the meeting point, the time, and any last details. Every message is stored linked to the request, so the whole conversation stays in one thread.

Message threads hang off the request: there is no general chat. Each thread belongs to one request, between the tourist and the provider on that request only.

How a message thread looks

Here is an example of a short thread on one request.

Example messages on a Mara safari request
Who Message
Tourist Hello David, we are two adults. Can we meet at the hotel gate at 6 in the morning?
Guide Yes, 6 in the morning works. Bring a jacket, it is cold at sunrise. I will bring water.
Tourist Thank you. See you at 6.

Messages are private: only the tourist and the provider on that request can see them. The administrator can read messages only to handle a reported problem.

How to message

  1. Log in and open one of your requests.
  2. Type your message in the box under the request and send it.
  3. The other side sees it in the same thread. New messages are marked unread until they are opened.

To send a real message, open one of your bookings on the live system — My requests — then open the booking and use the message box there.

Keep it on NativeGo

Agreeing details inside the thread keeps a record if anything is disputed later. It also keeps your phone number private until you choose to share it.
