NativeGo — A Simple Guide

What the website does, what the database stores, and how it all fits together.

What is NativeGo?

NativeGo connects tourists visiting Kenya with local people who offer services: safari guides, city tours, mountain guides, water sports, cultural experiences, fishing, and more. A tourist who does not know a trusted local guide finds one here; a local guide who has the skill but no easy way to be found is found here.

The three people who use it

RoleWho it isWhat they do
TouristA visitor to KenyaRegisters, searches for a guide, sends a request, messages the guide, leaves a review, reports a problem.
Local ProviderA Kenyan guide or hostRegisters, builds a profile, uploads documents to prove who they are, sets when they are free, accepts or declines requests.
AdministratorThe person who runs the siteLogs in, checks provider documents, approves or rejects providers, manages accounts, handles reports, sees statistics.
