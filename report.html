Report a problem

If a provider misbehaves — does not show up, misleads you, asks for payment outside NativeGo, or behaves in a rude or unsafe way — report them. The administrator reviews every report and decides what to do.

How to report

  1. Log in and open the provider's profile.
  2. Choose to report them and pick a reason: No-show, Misleading listing, Rude or unsafe behaviour, Asked for payment outside NativeGo, Fake profile, or Other.
  3. Write a short description of what happened and send it.
  4. The report is stored for the administrator. You get a confirmation and the case is queued for review.

To report a real provider, open their profile on the live system — for example Mara Savannah Safaris — and use the report button there.

What happens next

The administrator reads the report, looks at the messages and the request, and decides: warn the provider, suspend the account, or close the case with no action. Both sides are told the outcome. A report is not a review — it goes to the administrator, not onto the public profile.

If you are in danger right now, call the local police first. This form is for account problems, not emergencies.

Report, don't just review

A bad review warns other tourists. A report lets the administrator act — warn, suspend, or remove the account. Use the report when a rule was broken, not just when you were unhappy.
