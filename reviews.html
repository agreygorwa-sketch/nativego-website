Reviews

After a trip, a tourist rates the provider 1 to 5 stars and writes a comment. The review is stored, and the provider's average rating is updated. Reviews are how the next tourist decides whom to trust.

A review is only allowed after a completed booking. The review form opens only once the booking is marked completed, so every review on this site comes from a real trip. That is the transaction-verified rule: no completed booking, no review.

How to leave a review

  1. Finish the trip with your guide.
  2. Log in, open the provider's profile, and choose to review them.
  3. Pick a rating from 1 to 5 and write a short comment.
  4. Send it. Your review appears on the provider's profile and the average rating updates straight away.

One tourist can review one provider only once. That stops one person from flooding a profile with many reviews. To leave a review, open the provider on the live system — for example Mara Savannah Safaris — and use the review form there.

Why reviews matter

Reviews are the trust signal. A new tourist cannot meet a guide first, so they read what past tourists said. Honest reviews keep good guides busy and warn others about bad ones.
