Sign up

Create a free NativeGo account in about a minute. You can sign up as a tourist, to find and book local guides, or as a local provider, to offer your guiding services to visitors.

Two kinds of account

Tourist

Find a verified guide, send a request and book your trip.

Local provider

Offer your guiding services, set when you are free, and accept requests from visitors. Your profile stays hidden until an administrator checks your documents and approves you.

What you need

Just your name, an email address, and a password of at least 8 characters.

Providers also add their services, rates and a photo, and upload an identity document or certificate for verification.
