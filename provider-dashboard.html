For providers

If you are a Kenyan guide or host, NativeGo is how tourists find you. You register, build a profile, prove who you are, and take bookings. This page covers everything a provider does, requirement by requirement.

Your journey as a provider

Provider requirements and where they happen
Step What you do Requirement What happens
1. Register Create a provider account with your name, email, password, and the services you offer. FR-09 Your account is created, but your profile stays hidden until verification passes.
2. Build your profile Write your bio, languages, services, rates, and add photos. FR-10 Your profile is saved. You can edit it any time.
3. Upload documents Upload your ID, passport, certificate, or licence. FR-11 Files are checked for type, stored, and queued for the administrator to review.
4. Set availability Set the days and times you are free, with a short note if needed. FR-12 Your availability shows in search results, so tourists only request you when you are free.
5. Accept or decline When a request comes in, accept or decline it. FR-13 The request status updates and the tourist is told.
6. See your history Look back at your requests and bookings in date order. FR-14 Your full history is listed, so you can track past trips and earnings.

The trust gate

Your profile is hidden from tourists until the administrator approves your documents (FR-16). This is on purpose: tourists only see providers who have been checked. Once approved, you appear in search and tourists can request you.

To do all of this, open your dashboard on the live system: Provider dashboard. If you do not have an account yet, sign up as a provider.

What tourists see

Tourists see your business name, service, place, price, rating, and reviews — and whether you are verified. They do not see your documents or your private details.
